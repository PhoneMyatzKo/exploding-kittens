How to play

    Be the first to play your last card. The winner scores what everybody else is still holding.

    The deal

    Seven cards each. One card is turned face up to start the pile, and if it has a symbol on it, that symbol happens to the first player straight away.

    Your turn

    Play one card that matches the pile by colour or by symbol — or any wild. Can't play? Take one from the deck. If it plays, you may put it straight down; if it doesn't, your turn is over.

    The cards

    0–9
    Match the colour, or the same number in any colour.
    ⊘ Skip
    The next player loses their turn.
    ⇄ Reverse
    Play turns around and comes back the other way. With two players it simply comes straight back to you.
    +2 Draw Two
    The next player takes two cards and loses their turn. They do not stack — one Draw Two, one victim.
    Wild
    Playable on anything. You name the colour that has to be followed.
    Wild Draw Four
    Name the colour, and the next player takes four and loses their turn. You are only allowed this one when you have no card of the colour in force — see below.

    Calling the bluff

    A Wild Draw Four played while you did hold that colour is a bluff, and the player it lands on may challenge instead of drawing. They get to see your hand: if you bluffed, you take the four cards and their turn goes ahead as normal. If you were honest, they take six.

    Saying UNO

    Down to one card, say UNO! — the button by your hand. Forget, and anybody who notices before the next player has finished their turn can call you out, and you draw two.

    Running out of deck

    When the draw pile is gone, the pile of played cards is shuffled and becomes the new deck, leaving the top card where it is.

    Scoring

    Numbers are worth their face value, Skip, Reverse and Draw Two twenty each, and either wild fifty.
